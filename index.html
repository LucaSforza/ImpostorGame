Pocket Circle — free party games on one phone

Gather your friends, pick a game, and pass the phone. Free and open source, with no ads, accounts, subscriptions or tracking. English and Italian.

  • Impostore · 3–20 players. Secret words, bluffing and group votes to find hidden impostors.
  • Bomba · 2–20 players. Name answers to a topic before a hidden timer explodes.
  • Chi sono? · 3–20 players. See everyone’s identity except your own. Ask yes/no questions and make your guess.
  • Twit · 3–20 players. Estimate a number and predict whose answer will be farthest off.

Quattro giochi da festa gratuiti e open source, da giocare con gli amici su un solo telefono. Senza pubblicità, account, abbonamenti o tracciamento. Scegli un gioco, aggiungi i nomi e passa il telefono.

Profiles, photos, scores and active games stay in this browser. First visit needs a connection; offline reopening requires a completed cache download.
