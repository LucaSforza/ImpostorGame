## Description

Pocket Circle turns one phone into a pass-and-play party table. Choose a hidden-word bluff, a secret-timer word game, a “who am I?” guessing round, or a number-estimation game. Share the phone, keep private turns private, and let the group talk through the rest.

Four games support 2–20 players, depending on the game. The English/Italian app saves profiles, active sessions, and scores in the current browser. No account, ads, analytics, or game server. It is free, open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and works offline after the first visit and cache setup while the browser keeps its cache.

## Maker comment
