# Pocket Circle launch notes

Pocket Circle brings four pass-and-play games together in one private, local-first app: Impostore, Bomba, Chi sono?, and Twit. Share one phone, switch between English and Italian, and keep profiles, active games, and scores in the current browser.

- **Impostore:** bluff around a hidden word and vote to find the impostors.
- **Bomba:** race a secret 45–120 second deadline with spoken topic answers; the group makes the final call.
- **Chi sono?:** ask yes-or-no questions, then make one guess at your hidden identity.
- **Twit:** estimate a number and predict who will be farthest off; play 1, 3, 5, or 10 rounds.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or gameplay backend. Free and open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The GitHub Pages version can work offline after its first visit and cache setup while the browser retains its cache.
