# 15–30 second capture plan

Record the current app with temporary, fictional player names. Keep personal photos, browser notifications, and unrelated tabs out of frame. Show real gameplay and use on-screen text only where it helps; do not add claims that the recording does not demonstrate.

| Time | Shot | Caption or voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Catalog on a phone, showing the four cards. | “One phone. Four party games.” |
| 3–8 s | Impostore private reveal, then a quick cut to the group discussion/vote screen. | “Share a word. Find the impostors.” |
| 8–13 s | Bomba topic screen and a cut to group adjudication. | “Race a secret timer. Decide together.” |
| 13–18 s | Chi sono? private identities/questions, then Twit estimate and prediction. | “Guess who you are. Call the farthest guess.” |
| 18–23 s | English/Italian toggle or stats, then return to catalog. | “English and Italian. Your scores stay here.” |
| 23–27 s | End card with Pocket Circle and a readable URL. | “Play free: lucasforza.github.io/ImpostorGame” |

Aim for a 9:16 phone capture with readable UI. Capture a fresh, clean browser profile and avoid showing the address bar if it exposes account or personal information. Use a real session; do not fabricate player reactions or add fake review text. If recording is unavailable, a short sequence of the six provided screenshots is more accurate than suggesting footage was captured.
